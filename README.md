## Hello, I'm Arbaz 👋

I'm a founder, product builder and multidisciplinary digital professional based in Islamabad, Pakistan. I lead **[Ninja Creativity](https://ninjacreativity.com)** and **[Creative Craft](https://creativecraft.pk)** while working independently with businesses and international clients.

With more than seven years of experience, I combine **business development, UI/UX quality assurance, product thinking, customer support, marketing and visual design** to turn ideas into clear, useful and commercially focused digital experiences.

My strongest skill is seeing the complete picture: understanding the business goal, evaluating the user experience, finding quality issues and helping a product communicate its value more effectively.

## What I do

- **Business Development** — turning opportunities into clear product and service strategies
- **UI/UX Quality Assurance** — identifying usability problems, visual inconsistencies and experience gaps
- **Product Strategy** — shaping features, workflows, positioning and launch direction
- **UI/UX & Web Design** — creating clean, responsive and user-focused interfaces
- **Graphic & Brand Design** — developing identities, campaign visuals and marketing assets
- **Customer Experience** — improving support journeys, communication and user satisfaction
- **Digital Marketing** — connecting product value with the right audience and message
- **SaaS & Application Development** — building and guiding web, mobile and macOS products
